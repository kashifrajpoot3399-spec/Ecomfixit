FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

What does an Amazon agency do?

An Amazon agency runs the parts of your business that drive sales on Amazon: advertising, listing content and SEO, account health and growth planning, so you can focus on product and stock.

How do you lower a high ACoS?

We restructure campaigns by product and match type, add negative keywords, fix bids, move budget to search terms that convert and improve listings so more clicks turn into sales.

How do you improve my rankings?

We find the keywords your buyers use, build them into your listing, and support the most important ones with targeted ads. Sales from those keywords push your organic rank up.

What is included in the free audit?

We review your campaigns, search terms, listings and account health, then show you where money is being wasted and what to fix first.

How does onboarding work?

You fill in a short questionnaire, we have a kickoff call, and you add us as a user in Seller Central. Then we run a full audit and share the plan before we change anything.

Do you need full access to Seller Central?

No. You add us as a user with only the permissions the work needs. You stay the owner and can remove access at any time.

Do you send reports?

Yes. You get regular reports on sales, ad spend, ACoS, TACoS, ROAS and rankings, plus what we changed and what comes next.

What support can I expect?

You talk directly to the people running your account, on WhatsApp or email, with regular check-ins and clear answers.

Can you help manage inventory?

Yes. Inventory planning and FBA shipments are part of account management, so you stay in stock without overstocking.

What can I get for free before signing up?

A free audit of your account: where ad money is wasted, what is holding your listings back and what to fix first.

Can you help with Brand Registry?

Yes. We help you get through Brand Registry and then use it: A+ Content, a Storefront and Sponsored Brands ads.
